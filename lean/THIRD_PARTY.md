# Third-party sources

- **Lean and Mathlib:** installed through the exact toolchain and dependency
  pins; their upstream licenses and authorship remain unchanged.
- **Classical prime number theorem:** the required local port of
  [PrimeNumberTheoremAnd](https://github.com/AlexKontorovich/PrimeNumberTheoremAnd)
  retains its [Apache-2.0 license](PrimeGapNormality/ClassicalPNT/LICENSE),
  [upstream citation](PrimeGapNormality/ClassicalPNT/CITATION.upstream.cff),
  and [port provenance](PrimeGapNormality/ClassicalPNT/PROVENANCE.md).

The author's original code is licensed under [Apache 2.0](../LICENSE);
the paper is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](../paper/LICENSE). These third-party
notices do not attribute this paper's normality results to dependency authors.
